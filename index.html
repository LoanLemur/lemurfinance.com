The Platform

Patient & Consumer Financing

Your patients need a procedure but can't pay upfront. We give them flexible monthly plans at checkout — and pay you in full within days. You take zero credit risk. Built for dental, veterinary, and similar practices.

In-House Lending

For merchants who want to offer financing directly to their customers without the operational overhead. Automated ACH debits timed to customer payroll cycles, credit assessment that goes beyond FICO, and portfolio management. No lending operation required.

Automated Collections

Past-due accounts sitting in a spreadsheet aren't recovering themselves. Our system contacts delinquent customers, negotiates payment plans, and collects — all without your team picking up the phone.
