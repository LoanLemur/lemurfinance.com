## Design Context

### Users
Mid-market business operators arriving via referral. They've heard about Lemur from someone they trust and are visiting to validate credibility before taking a meeting. The site's job is to make them think "these people are legit."

### Brand Personality
Precise, rigorous, confident. The tone is a senior engineer who builds things that work — competent without being showy. Not luxury, not playful. Engineered.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone:** Swiss Precision — clean, systematic, engineered
- **Theme:** Light mode. Near-white background with one dark section (team). Deep navy accent used sparingly.
- **Typography:** Sora (display, geometric sans) + Satoshi (body, clean sans). Hierarchy through weight contrast (light 300 headlines, 600 bold for emphasis).
- **Anti-references:** Dark mode + gold accent + serif (atomiccommit.com template), cheerful SMB SaaS, glassmorphism, gradient text
- **References:** Stripe's design rigor, Swiss International Style posters, clean engineering documentation

### Design Principles
1. **The grid is the design** — No decorative elements. Structure, typography, and whitespace do all the work.
2. **One accent, used sparingly** — Deep navy (#1a3a5c) is the only color beyond neutrals and the brand yellow. It appears on CTAs and links, nowhere else.
3. **Light with one dark moment** — The team section is the sole dark section, creating a single dramatic contrast point. Everything else is light.
4. **Weight, not decoration** — Hierarchy comes from font weight (300 vs 600), size, and spacing. No gold lines, no grain textures, no glowing effects.
5. **Precision signals credibility** — Tight alignment, consistent spacing, systematic type scale. The care in the details IS the message.
